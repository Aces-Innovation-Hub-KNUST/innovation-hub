# Governance and responsibilities

This document describes the organizational roles relevant to the Hub's software and project-development repository. It does not create leadership positions or replace ACES-approved governance.

## Innovation Hub board

The Innovation Hub board provides organizational direction for the Hub's activities and project pipeline under the applicable ACES arrangements. It is responsible for keeping plans, decisions, and handover information aligned with those arrangements.

## Software & Hackathons responsibility

The Software & Hackathons board member is responsible for the software hackathon, software skill sprints, and the Hub GitHub organization. This is a coordination and leadership responsibility; it does not mean that one board member personally performs every operational task.

## Volunteer organizing committees

Flagship events are delivered by volunteer organizing committees led by the relevant board member. Committees may coordinate event preparation and delivery. Their scope and membership should follow approved arrangements; this repository does not invent named positions or assign individuals.

## Financial Secretary

Coordinate financial planning, approvals, records, and handling of funds with the ACES Financial Secretary in accordance with applicable ACES procedures. Do not treat this repository as a financial ledger or publish confidential account information.

## Grants Desk and project funding

The plan identifies grants and project funding as part of the Hub's work. Coordinate funding opportunities and proposals with the Grants Desk under the approved organizational process. Project teams may pursue suitable grants, sponsorships, competitions, or related opportunities; no funding is guaranteed.

## Repository ownership

The Hub GitHub organization and this repository are organizational resources. They are not the personal property of a board member, volunteer, or project team. Access, administrative control, and transfer of ownership should follow ACES-approved processes and be handed over to successor leadership.
