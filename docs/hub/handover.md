# Handover expectations

This repository and its documentation are ACES Innovation Hub organizational assets. They should remain usable when individual student leaders or project members leave their roles.

## At each leadership transition

- Transfer repository and organization administration through the approved ACES process; do not rely on a personal account as the sole point of control.
- Review access and remove access that is no longer needed.
- Update role-based documentation and identify any unresolved decisions without inventing successors or contacts.
- Hand over current plans, project stages, milestones, blockers, and upcoming dates.
- Distinguish completed work from planned or ongoing work.
- Transfer relevant event and funding records through the appropriate approved channels; keep confidential financial and personal information out of this repository.
- Confirm that credentials are not stored in the repository. Rotate credentials when access changes or exposure is suspected.
- Review open issues and pull requests and record any context needed to continue them.
- Check that links, templates, and timeline information remain current.

## Recordkeeping

Document decisions and processes at the level needed for continuity. Keep records factual and proportionate. Do not store passwords, API keys, private credentials, or unnecessary personal information in project files.
