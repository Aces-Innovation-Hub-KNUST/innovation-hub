# Hackathon team formation

The 2026/2027 plan anticipates a Software Hackathon team formation night approximately one week before the planned **2–4 April 2027** event. The exact date and format are not specified and must be confirmed by organizers.

## Planning principles

- Help participants form teams of 3–4.
- Encourage mixed skill levels.
- Connect teams with real campus or community problems.
- Make it possible for participants to describe their interests, skills, and areas where they want to learn.
- Share confirmed event information and expectations once available.

Use the [hackathon project template](../../templates/hackathon-project.md) to capture a team's problem, proposed solution, intended demo, and support needs. Do not collect unnecessary personal information or publish attendee lists without authorization.
